The Rules

🦵

48h leg rule. Quad day (D2), hinge day (D4), and power/speed day (D6) are each 2 full days apart — never load the legs hard two days running.

⏱

60-min target, 70-min cap on weekdays. Sunday yoga is the exception — take as long as feels good.

🔋

Energy management. Heavy compound lifts always come before cardio, except Day 5. Never burn your legs on cardio then try to lift heavy.

📈

Progressive overload. Hit the top of the rep range clean on all sets → add a small amount of weight next session.

⚖️

Form over weight. Shoulders rolling on a press? Back arching on a hinge? Drop the weight.
