The Rules

⏱

90-min hard cap. If you're going over, cut a set — not a cooldown. Cortisol past 90 min fights your goals.

🔋

Energy management. Heavy compound lifts always come before cardio (except Day 3). Never burn your CNS on cardio then try to lift heavy.

📈

Progressive overload. Hit 10 reps clean this week → go for 11 or add 1kg next week. Track it.

⚖️

Form over weight. Shoulders rolling on a press? Back arching on a deadlift? Drop the weight.

➕

The Plus rule. On every press, lock out fully and reach shoulders forward at the top. Every rep.
